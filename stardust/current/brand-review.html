Tensions synthesized

T-scale

Type scale is ad-hoc (54 → 42 → 37.5 → 34 → 30 → 24 → 19.2 px, no consistent ratio). Direct will need to decide whether the target adopts a modular scale.

T-logo-variants

Only one logo variant captured (header img, gold wordmark SVG). The redesign will need a monochrome / inverted variant set; direct should plan that.

T-no-tokens

No CSS custom properties defined on :root (Tailwind v3 compiled utilities). The current site has no design-token layer; the migration target will introduce tokens.

T-img-alt-empty

60% of images (102/170) carry empty alt text — mostly decorative cloud/sparkle layers, but also some product imagery. Accessibility and content-sourcing decision.

T-theme-split (agent)

Two themes on one origin: sling (Inter + Work Sans 600, gold-gradient text, glass pills) vs gpp_promo_theme on /xp/golden-ticket/ (Work Sans 700, flat #eec60d pill with purple drop edge, cream panel). Replica must keep both or register the difference.

T-content-width (agent)

contentMaxWidth differs per archetype: 739 (home), fluid (about), 800 in a 1268 shell (legal), 1536 (golden-ticket) — cap-probe register entries.

T-intro-gate (agent)

Home opens (first session) on a hat-portal video intro that makes the page inert until dismissed; content capture needed the post-intro state.
