UNWRAPPING YOUR QUESTIONS

1. When will Wonka be on shelves?

Wonka treats won't just launch once. They'll come and go throughout the year, appearing as seasonal, limited-edition creations before disappearing again... only to return in new forms. The first wave arrives Fall 2026, so keep checking back for what's currently on shelves.

2. What kind of treats can I expect?

A whole world of confections: chocolate, sugar candy, and ice cream creations, each dreamed up with signature Wonka flavors, textures and iconic shapes.

3. Where can I find Wonka products?

Look for Wonka wherever you buy treats today. Availability is limited to key seasons, so if you don’t see it today, check back soon.

4. Is Wonka a Ferrero brand?

Yes. Ferrero holds the exclusive global rights to Wonka in confectionery, following its acquisition of Nestlé's US confectionery business in 2018.

5. Is this connected to the new Netflix film and series?

Ferrero's Wonka and Netflix are bringing the world of Wonka to life together, on screen and on shelf. We can't share more just yet, but keep an eye out... the Wonka world has a way of turning up in surprising places.

6. Who are these products for?

Wonka is a treat for anyone young at heart who grew up loving the franchise and wants to rediscover that sense of wonder. We do not market directly to children.

7. Where are Wonka products made?

Across Ferrero's global manufacturing network, ensuring the same quality and craftsmanship Ferrero is known for worldwide.
